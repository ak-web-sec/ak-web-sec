📋 Our Build Process

  • Discovery & scope (1–2 days) We define features, user flows, platforms, and technical requirements. We deliver a detailed scope document and fixed quote.
  • Wireframing & prototyping (3–5 days) Interactive prototypes of every screen. You can tap through the app before a single line of code is written.
  • UI design & approval (5–7 days) High-fidelity designs for every screen, animation specs, and a design system. Two revision rounds included.
  • Development sprints (2–6 weeks) Agile sprints with weekly demos. You see progress in real time via TestFlight (iOS) or APK (Android).
  • Testing & QA (1 week) Functional testing, performance testing, security review, and device compatibility across 10+ screen sizes.
  • Launch & store submission (3–5 days) App store assets prepared, submission handled, and launch monitoring for the first 48 hours post-release.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is the price determined?

Price depends on number of screens, platform(s), backend complexity, third-party integrations, and design requirements. A simple 5-screen app starts at R15,000. Complex apps with real-time features, payment processing, and admin dashboards range up to R50,000+.

Do I need a maintenance plan?

No — but strongly recommended. OS updates (iOS/Android) can break apps. Our maintenance plans handle compatibility updates, bug fixes, and security patches automatically.

Can you update an existing app?

Yes. We can take over existing React Native, Flutter, or native apps. We'll audit the codebase first and give you an honest assessment of what needs fixing vs. rebuilding.

Who owns the app and code?

You do. We deliver full source code, design files, and app store credentials. We can also sign an IP transfer agreement if required.
