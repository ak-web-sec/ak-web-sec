Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

We take security seriously. If you discover a vulnerability in our systems, we want to know.

Our Commitment

AKWEBSEC is committed to ensuring the security and integrity of our systems and data. We value the security research community and believe that responsible disclosure is the best way to keep our clients and infrastructure safe.

Scope

This policy applies to the following domains and services:

  • akwebsec.co.za and all subdomains
  • Our public-facing APIs and web applications
  • Mobile applications published under the AKWEBSEC name

How to Report

Send vulnerability reports to security@akwebsec.co.za. Please include:

  • A clear description of the vulnerability
  • Steps to reproduce (proof of concept where possible)
  • The potential impact and affected systems
  • Your contact details for follow-up questions

What We Promise

  • Acknowledgment: We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours.
  • Assessment: We will investigate and validate the issue promptly.
  • Remediation: We aim to remediate validated vulnerabilities within 90 days, depending on severity and complexity.
  • Safe Harbor: We will not take legal action against researchers who follow this policy and act in good faith.

Rules of Engagement

To ensure responsible disclosure:

  • Do not access, modify, or delete data belonging to others.
  • Do not perform actions that could degrade our services (DoS).
  • Do not use social engineering or physical attacks.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

Recognition

With your permission, we will publicly acknowledge researchers who report valid vulnerabilities in our security hall of fame (coming soon).
