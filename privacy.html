Privacy Policy

How we handle your data. Plain language, no surprises.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

1. Who We Are

AKWEBSEC is a South African cybersecurity and secure development practice operated by Abdurraheem Khan. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website or services.

2. What We Collect

We collect only what is necessary:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, phone number, and company name when you fill out our contact form or request a security snapshot.
  • Website data: Domain names and IP addresses you authorize us to test.
  • Usage data: Anonymous analytics via Cloudflare Web Analytics (no cookies, no personal tracking).

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook pixels, or any third-party tracking that shares data across sites.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide the services you requested.
  • To deliver security reports and monitoring alerts.
  • To comply with legal obligations and POPIA requirements.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.

4. Data Storage and Security

All client data is stored securely. Pentest reports are shared via encrypted channels only. We retain client data for the duration of the engagement plus one year for compliance purposes, after which it is securely deleted.

5. Your Rights (POPIA)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@akwebsec.co.za.

6. Cookies

We use a single cookie to remember your cookie consent preference. We do not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or profiling.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email hello@akwebsec.co.za or WhatsApp +27 78 267 7182.
