Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our security monitoring and penetration testing services.

Getting Started

What exactly do you monitor?
We monitor your websites, web applications, and APIs for security vulnerabilities, uptime, SSL certificate health, and subdomain security. Specifically, we check for: known CVEs (security flaws in software you use), exposed admin panels or sensitive files, missing security headers, weak SSL/TLS configurations, subdomain takeover risks, and website downtime. Every day, our systems run thousands of automated checks against your targets.
Do you need access to my server or source code?
No. Our monitoring is entirely external — just like a real attacker would see your site. We only need your website URLs (like https://yourcompany.com). We do not need server access, login credentials, or source code. This is actually an advantage: we see exactly what an attacker sees, which means if we find a vulnerability, a real attacker could too.
How long does it take to get started?
Once you sign up and provide your target URLs, we configure monitoring within 24–48 hours. You'll receive a "monitoring active" confirmation, and your first weekly summary arrives the following Monday. If you choose the R250 deposit option, your partial report is delivered within 48 hours of payment.
Is there a contract or minimum term?
Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice. Annual plans are paid upfront and offer 2 months free plus a free penetration test. We don't lock you into long-term contracts — we believe our service should be good enough that you want to stay.

The R250 Deposit Option

How does the R250 deposit work?
It's simple: you pay R250 upfront, we run a targeted security scan on your website within 48 hours, and you receive a partial report showing the top 3–5 findings. If you want the full detailed report, you pay an additional R250 (or subscribe to a monthly plan). If we find zero critical or high-severity issues, we refund your R250 in full. No risk, no commitment.
What if you don't find anything?
If our scan finds zero critical or high-severity issues, we refund your R250 deposit. Period. We're confident we'll find value, but if your security is genuinely solid, you don't pay. This is our Zero-Finding Guarantee.
Can I skip the deposit and go straight to a monthly plan?
Absolutely. The deposit option is for skeptical prospects who want proof before committing. If you're ready to start, you can sign up for any monthly plan immediately — no deposit needed.

Security & Privacy

Is your scanning safe? Will it break my website?
Our automated scans are designed to be non-intrusive. We use the same tools and techniques that security professionals worldwide use for reconnaissance. However, no scan is 100% risk-free. That's why we require a signed service agreement before scanning, and we recommend that you maintain backups. If you have a particularly fragile or legacy system, let us know and we'll tune the scan intensity.
Where is my data stored?
Scan results and client data are stored encrypted on our secure servers. We follow POPIA principles: we only process data you authorize, we store it encrypted at rest and in transit, and we delete it 30 days after your contract ends. We never share your data with third parties, and we do not use your scan results for marketing without explicit consent.
What happens if you find a critical vulnerability?
If our monitoring detects a critical or high-severity issue, you receive an immediate alert via email (and Telegram for Professional tier clients). The alert includes: what we found, why it matters, how severe it is, and what you should do about it. We do not disclose vulnerabilities to anyone except your designated contacts. For Sentinel Professional clients, we also provide phone notification for critical findings.
Do you fix the vulnerabilities you find?
We provide detailed remediation guidance — step-by-step instructions on how to fix each issue. However, we do not implement fixes ourselves (we're not your developers). If you don't have in-house technical expertise, we can refer you to trusted development partners, or for Sentinel Professional clients, we offer remediation advisory calls to help your team understand what needs to be done.

Technical

What technologies can you scan?
We can scan any website or web application regardless of technology stack: WordPress, Shopify, React, Angular, Vue, Node.js, PHP, Python/Django, Ruby on Rails, Java, .NET — it doesn't matter. Our scanners detect the technology automatically and adjust their checks accordingly. We also test APIs (REST and GraphQL) and mobile app backends.
What's the difference between a scan and a pentest?
A vulnerability scan is automated. It checks for known issues like outdated software, missing patches, and misconfigurations. It's fast, runs daily, and catches the "low-hanging fruit."

A penetration test includes manual work by a human security analyst who thinks creatively about how to break your specific application. It finds business logic flaws, authentication bypasses, and complex vulnerabilities that automation misses.

Our monthly plans include continuous automated scanning. Our one-off pentest service (or free annual pentest for annual subscribers) adds the human expertise layer.
Will your scans trigger my firewall or WAF?
Possibly. Some Web Application Firewalls (WAFs) may flag automated scanning traffic. We provide a fixed IP address for our scanning servers that you can whitelist with your hosting provider or firewall. If whitelisting isn't possible, we can adjust scan timing and intensity to reduce the chance of being blocked.
How is this different from my hosting provider's security?
Hosting providers typically secure the server (the computer your website runs on). They patch the operating system, manage the firewall, and ensure the physical hardware is safe. We secure the application (your actual website code, database, and user-facing features). A server can be perfectly secure while the application running on it is full of vulnerabilities. You need both layers.
