Penetration Testing

Manual simulated attacks against your web applications, APIs, and infrastructure. OWASP-aligned methodology with actionable reports.

Our Methodology

Every engagement follows a structured approach based on OWASP and industry best practices.

1

Reconnaissance

We map your attack surface — domains, subdomains, APIs, and exposed services. We systematically map the agreed attack surface before testing.

2

Vulnerability Discovery

Automated scanning plus manual testing for business logic flaws, authentication bypasses, and chained attack paths that scanners miss.

3

Exploitation & Validation

We safely exploit confirmed vulnerabilities to demonstrate real impact. Every finding includes evidence and a clear risk rating.

4

Reporting & Remediation

You receive a detailed report with CVSS scores, step-by-step reproduction instructions, and prioritized remediation guidance.

Authorization Required

Before we begin any security assessment, we require written confirmation that you are the owner or authorized representative of the target systems. This protects both parties and ensures all testing is legal and ethical.

By submitting a pentest request, you confirm that you have the legal authority to authorize security testing against the specified domain or IP address.

Scope & Rules of Engagement

  • In scope: Domains, subdomains, web applications, and APIs explicitly agreed upon in the engagement letter.
  • Out of scope: Social engineering, physical security testing, and denial-of-service attacks.
  • Testing hours: Business hours (08:00–17:00 SAST) unless otherwise agreed.
  • Emergency contact: Critical findings are reported immediately via email and WhatsApp.
