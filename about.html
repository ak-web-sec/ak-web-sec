"Every South African business deserves to know if they are vulnerable — before attackers do. We make that possible with transparent pricing, continuous monitoring, and clear communication."

AKWEBSEC was founded to solve a simple problem: South African SMEs need security, but cannot afford R50,000+ annual retainers or offshore consultants who do not understand local regulations like POPIA.

We combine automated vulnerability scanning with manual penetration testing to give you coverage that rivals enterprise solutions — at a fraction of the cost. And because we are local, we understand the unique challenges facing South African businesses, from load shedding resilience to POPIA compliance.
