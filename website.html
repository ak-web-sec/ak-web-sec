📋 Our Build Process

  • Discovery call (30 min) We learn about your business, goals, target audience, and competitors. We discuss features, pages, and design preferences.
  • Wireframe & mockup Within 48 hours, you receive a wireframe showing page structure and a visual mockup of the homepage design.
  • Development sprint We build your site over 1–2 weeks depending on complexity. You get a private preview link to track progress.
  • Review & revisions Two rounds of revisions included. We tweak colours, text, images, and layout based on your feedback.
  • Launch & handover Site deployed to your domain. We train you on the CMS (if applicable) and hand over all credentials and source files.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How is the price determined?

Price depends on number of pages, complexity of design, integrations needed (booking systems, payment gateways, etc.), and whether you need a CMS. A simple 5-page brochure site starts at R5,000. E-commerce or custom web apps go up to R10,000+.

Do I need a maintenance plan?

No — the maintenance plan is optional. But without it, you're responsible for updates, security patches, and backups. Our plans start at R250/month and include bug fixes and content updates.

What if I already have a website?

We can redesign or rebuild your existing site. We also offer maintenance-only plans if you just need someone to keep your current site updated and secure.

Do you do e-commerce?

Yes. We build WooCommerce, Shopify, and custom e-commerce sites with PayFast integration for South African payments.
