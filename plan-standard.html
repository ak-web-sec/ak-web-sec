❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When do I get my free pentest?
Annual subscribers receive their free pentest around their anniversary month (12 months after signup). We'll contact you 2 weeks before to schedule a convenient time. The pentest includes full reconnaissance, automated and manual testing, and a detailed PDF report with a follow-up call.
Can I downgrade to Basic later?
Yes, you can downgrade at any time with 30 days' notice. Your monitoring continues uninterrupted, but you'll lose access to monthly reports, backup verification, and the additional target domains.
What does the monthly PDF report look like?
It's a professionally formatted PDF (typically 5–15 pages) with: an executive summary for non-technical stakeholders, a findings table ranked by severity, detailed descriptions of each issue with proof-of-concept screenshots, plain-English explanations of business impact, step-by-step remediation guidance, an uptime summary, and a security posture trend chart comparing to previous months.
How does backup verification work?
You tell us where your backups are stored (e.g., a specific folder, S3 bucket, or backup service). We check daily that: a new backup file exists, the file size is reasonable (not 0 bytes or suspiciously small), the timestamp is recent, and the file is accessible. If any check fails, you get an immediate alert. We do NOT access the content of your backups — only verify their existence and basic metadata.
