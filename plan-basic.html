Security Monitoring Plan

Watchdog Basic

R500/month

Essential security monitoring for small businesses, brochure sites, and personal projects. Know if you're vulnerable before the bad guys do.

📋 What's Included

  • Daily automated vulnerability scan Our systems scan your target URL every day using 10,000+ security checks against known CVEs, misconfigurations, and exposed services.
  • Uptime monitoring (60-second intervals) We check if your site is up every minute, 24/7. If it goes down, you know within 60 seconds.
  • Weekly email summary Every Monday morning, a concise email summarizing the week's scan results, uptime stats, and any issues found.
  • SSL certificate monitoring We alert you 30 days before your SSL certificate expires, and flag weak cipher suites or configuration issues.
  • 1 target domain included One primary domain (e.g., https://yourcompany.co.za). Additional domains available at R200/month each.
  • Instant critical alerts If we find a critical or high-severity vulnerability, you get an immediate email alert — no waiting for the weekly summary.
  • Basic security headers check We verify that essential security headers (HSTS, X-Frame-Options) are present and correctly configured.

🚫 What's NOT Included

  • Monthly PDF report Upgrade to Guardian Standard for detailed monthly PDF reports with full findings, charts, and remediation guidance.
  • Backup verification We don't check if your backups are running or restorable. Upgrade to Standard for daily backup verification.
  • Dependency vulnerability checks We don't specifically check if your JavaScript libraries, npm packages, or WordPress plugins have known CVEs. Upgrade to Standard.
  • Subdomain monitoring We only monitor the single domain you specify. Hidden subdomains or newly created ones are not tracked. Upgrade to Standard.
  • Manual penetration testing This plan is fully automated. Human-led pentests are available as one-off services or included free with annual Standard/Professional plans.
  • Phone support Email support only, with a 48-hour response time. Upgrade to Professional for phone support and 24-hour SLA.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade later?
Yes. You can upgrade to Guardian Standard or Sentinel Professional at any time. We'll prorate the difference and your monitoring continues without interruption.
What if I have multiple websites?
Watchdog Basic includes 1 target domain. Additional domains are R200/month each. Alternatively, Guardian Standard includes up to 3 domains and may be more cost-effective.
Is there a setup fee?
No setup fee for monthly plans. Annual plans are paid upfront and include 2 months free. The R250 deposit option has no additional fees.
Can I cancel anytime?
Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice. Annual plans are non-refundable but can be transferred to another entity.
