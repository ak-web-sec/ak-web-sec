Why We Exist

Small and medium businesses are disproportionately targeted by cyber attacks, yet most lack dedicated security resources. The average data breach costs South African businesses millions in recovery, fines, and reputational damage.

AKWEBSEC was built to bridge that gap. We combine industry-standard scanning tools with smart automation to deliver continuous security monitoring at a price that makes sense for local businesses.

We're South African based, operate in South African time, and understand the local threat landscape. No offshore call centers. No confusing jargon. Just clear, actionable security intelligence.
