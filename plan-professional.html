🔍 Quarterly Manual Penetration Tests

Every 3 months, a human security analyst performs a full OWASP Top 10 assessment of your application. This catches the subtle, creative vulnerabilities that automated tools miss.

  • Full reconnaissance and footprinting We map your entire attack surface: subdomains, APIs, cloud storage, exposed services, and third-party integrations.
  • Automated + manual testing We run our standard automated suite, then spend hours manually testing for business logic flaws, authentication bypasses, privilege escalation, and chained attack vectors.
  • Detailed PDF report with evidence Each quarterly report includes: executive summary, full findings with CVSS scores, proof-of-concept screenshots and videos, business impact analysis, and prioritized remediation roadmap.
  • Remediation advisory call A 30-minute call with your team to walk through findings, answer questions, and help prioritize fixes. This is included with every quarterly report.

🇿🇦 POPIA Compliance Readiness

South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act requires businesses to implement "appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures" to protect personal data. We help you meet and document these requirements.

  • Technical safeguards assessment We audit your HTTPS implementation, access controls, encryption practices, and data handling procedures against POPIA's security safeguard requirements.
  • Compliance documentation We provide a written assessment document you can show to auditors, regulators, or clients demonstrating your technical security measures.
  • Gap analysis and roadmap If gaps exist, we provide a prioritized roadmap to address them. We don't write privacy policies (you need a lawyer), but we ensure the technical side is covered.
  • Quarterly compliance reviews As part of each quarterly pentest, we re-assess your POPIA compliance posture and update documentation to reflect changes.

🌑 Dark Web Monitoring

We continuously monitor dark web forums, paste sites, and breach databases for mentions of your domain, email addresses, or brand. If your data appears in a breach dump, you'll know before your customers do.

  • Domain mention alerts If your domain name appears in dark web marketplaces, hacking forums, or breach dumps, you get an immediate alert with context.
  • Email breach detection We check if email addresses @yourdomain.com appear in known data breaches (using Have I Been Pwned and other sources).
  • Credential leak monitoring If usernames or passwords associated with your domain are leaked, we alert you immediately so you can force password resets.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this overkill for a small business?
Probably. Sentinel Professional is designed for businesses that handle sensitive data (financial, medical, legal) or have compliance obligations. If you're a small brochure site or local shop, Guardian Standard is likely sufficient. We're honest about this — we'll tell you if Professional is more than you need.
What industries is this designed for?
Fintech and payment processors, healthcare providers (medical practices, telehealth), legal firms handling client data, insurance companies, e-commerce stores processing payments, SaaS companies with user data, and any business subject to POPIA compliance audits.
Can we customize the quarterly pentest scope?
Absolutely. Before each quarterly pentest, we discuss your current priorities. Want us to focus on your new API endpoint? Test your mobile app backend? Review your admin panel's access controls? Just tell us. The pentest is tailored to your evolving needs.
Do you provide compliance certificates?
We provide a compliance readiness assessment document that details your technical security measures and how they align with POPIA requirements. This is not a formal "certificate" (no such thing exists for POPIA yet), but it is a valuable document for auditors, clients, and insurers. For formal certifications like ISO 27001 or SOC 2, we can refer you to specialized partners.
